How the calculator works +

Investment = vehicle count × entered bike cost. Fixed: Green Drift Urban uses the previously selected reference rate of 51% total net return over 36 months. Urban Plus uses 46% for up to 100 vehicles and 51% above 100. Total receipts = investment × (1 + total return rate). Monthly equivalent = total receipts ÷ 36; the actual payment schedule needs confirmation. Revenue Sharing: estimated monthly payout = vehicles × monthly distributable revenue per vehicle × 60% investor share. Green Drift receives the remaining 40%. Total receipts = estimated monthly payout × 36. Net gain / loss = total receipts − investment. Net return = net gain / loss ÷ investment × 100. Revenue Sharing starts at 50 vehicles; a Hub starts at 100, for either return model. No additional capital refund is assumed. These calculations exclude investor taxes, financing costs and changes to timing or fleet performance. Revenue Sharing is variable and all final terms require an agreement.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Big decisions start with the right information.

What does Green Drift do?+

Green Drift operates an EV fleet rental business in Kurla, Mumbai, helping riders access electric vehicles for last-mile work. Its vision deck outlines future engineering, energy and service capabilities.

Is ₹1,25,000 a guaranteed monthly return?+

No. This is an indicative opportunity figure provided by Green Drift. Applicable capital, fleet size, costs, utilisation assumptions, payout conditions and investment risks need to be explained and confirmed in a written agreement.

How are the investor and FOCO models different?+

The investor model explores capital participation in the fleet business. FOCO means Franchise Owned, Company Operated. The precise ownership, capital, revenue and responsibility structure for each model should be reviewed with the team before a decision.

Which cities are operating today?+

Green Drift currently operates in Kurla, Mumbai. Delhi and Bengaluru are planned hubs targeted by the end of 2028. Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow and Jaipur form part of the broader expansion ambition.

What should I ask for before investing?+

Request company documentation, investment and operating cost details, asset ownership terms, payout calculations, risk disclosures, reporting arrangements and exit provisions. Review the agreement carefully before committing funds.

Can I rent a scooter as a rider?+

Yes. Choose your preferred colour, pickup date and number of weeks in our weekly rental booking flow. Send your request to the Mumbai team to confirm availability and rental terms.
