Your privacy matters.

This website introduces GreenDrift Tech Pvt. Ltd., its fleet rental business and partnership opportunities.

Enquiry details

Contact and investment enquiries are saved only after you submit the form with consent. Rental requests are prepared for you to review and send in WhatsApp. These flows are described below.

Contact enquiries

When you request a conversation, we save your contact details, city, selected interest and message in Green Drift’s hosted enquiry storage so the team can respond. We do not reserve a call time automatically. Contact +91 81091 05582 to ask about your enquiry or request deletion.

Investment enquiries

When you register your investment interest, we save your name, phone number, email, city, message and calculator scenario to Green Drift’s hosted enquiry storage. The team uses this information to respond to your enquiry and explain the opportunity. These records are not publicly accessible. Do not submit bank details, payment information or identity documents. Contact +91 81091 05582 to ask about your enquiry or request deletion.

Weekly rental requests

The rental form prepares your details in your browser. Continuing on WhatsApp opens a prefilled message to Green Drift at +91 81091 05582. You review and send it yourself. WhatsApp processes information according to its own privacy policy. Green Drift receives your request only when you send it. No online payment is collected.

Marketplace accounts and seller applications

Marketplace sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email through Resend. We store your account email, hashed authentication codes and hashed sessions in our Neon database. An essential secure session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited using hashed email and network identifiers. Your marketplace cart is saved on your device.

If you apply as a seller, we store your business name, contact details, city, optional GSTIN and business description for review by Green Drift. Approved product information, business name and city are visible to marketplace visitors. Product submissions and edits require approval before publication.

When you send an order request with consent, your name, phone number and delivery address are shared with that product’s seller and Green Drift to discuss the request. Requests and their status are stored in our database. No payment details are collected by the marketplace at this stage. Seller-provided images may load from external image hosts; those hosts receive normal browser connection information. Contact contact@greendrift.in to ask about your marketplace data or request deletion.

Contacting us

Calling the team uses your device’s phone service. You can reach Green Drift on +91 81091 05582 with privacy questions or to discuss an opportunity.

Preferences and external services

Your light or dark theme preference is saved in local browser storage. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. The site does not include advertising trackers or analytics.

Downloaded files

The company vision deck downloads to your device. Handle any downloaded files with care.
